=== v1.9 bench topology, established Phase 0 ===

  syd (this host)                    RD03v2 dev bench                tiny (RPi)
  enx00e04c125990  --- wired --->    lan3 (1000M, carrier=1)
  192.168.1.50/24 (STATIC)           br-lan 192.168.1.1
  in netns "bench"                   phy1-ap0 (5 GHz ch36)  <-- wifi --  wlan0
                                                                         192.168.1.146
                                                                         (DHCP)

Control paths (deliberately NOT the links under test):
  - bench   : wired, via scripts/bsh -> ip netns exec bench -> ssh root@192.168.1.1
  - tiny    : wired, br0/eth0 192.168.100.145 (NOT wlan0). A bench reflash drops
              tiny's wifi; its control path must not ride on it.

Addressing decisions:
  - syd's bench-side address is STATIC 192.168.1.50/24, outside the bench DHCP
    pool (.100-.249, 12h lease). A lease here can expire or be reassigned
    mid-flash; the control path must not depend on one.
  - Stale addresses removed during setup: 192.168.2.101/24 (syd wired),
    192.168.2.60/24 (tiny wlan0). Both were leftovers from an earlier bench
    topology and were why the wired link first appeared "not cabled" - the cable
    was fine, the L3 addressing was simply on a network nobody served.

tiny wifi notes:
  - wlan0 was NM-"unmanaged" and held by a stray wpa_supplicant (PID 4223,
    /run/wifi-client-wlan0.conf) already pinned to BSSID xx:xx:xx:xx:xx:xx, i.e.
    someone had set this up before. Retired it so NetworkManager owns wlan0.
  - A plain `nmcli dev wifi connect ... bssid <5GHz>` still associated to 2.4 GHz
    (phy0-ap0). Pinning the PROFILE with `802-11-wireless.band a` plus the bssid
    is what actually holds it on 5 GHz.
  - Associated: ch36 (5180 MHz), -7 dBm at the AP, tx 325 Mbit/s VHT-MCS 7
    80 MHz VHT-NSS 1.

those ran wifi-to-router (terminated). This is the load generator for finding #1.
