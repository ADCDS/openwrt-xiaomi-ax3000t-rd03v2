How to configure? 
Overwrite the /etc/modules.d/32-qca-nss-drv with following contents then reboot

qca-nss-drv meminfo_user_config="<core_id, name, memory_type>, .."

For example, <1, h2n_rings, IMEM> stands for: h2n_rings of core 1 is on IMEM
Note:UTCM_SHARED cannot be used for n2h_rings, h2n_rings and debug_log_boot_desc.
