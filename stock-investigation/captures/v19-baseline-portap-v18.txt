Frozen across all three samples. These are cumulative-since-boot counters that
are NOT advancing at idle on a box with 8 d 20 h uptime, so the large absolute
values are historical (most plausibly NSS init, before the host pool was
populated) rather than ongoing starvation.

Consequences for finding #1:
  - The doc's "stock sat at a constant 99" is an ABSOLUTE value on stock's own
    firmware. Our v1.8 idles at 45864 with the CURRENT 8704-buffer pool, so the
    absolute number is not comparable across the two firmwares and cannot be
    the pass/fail test.
  - The kill criterion must therefore be the DELTA measured across a load run
    on OUR build: sample before, drive forwarded traffic, sample after. A pool
    that is too small shows up as the delta growing once the pool is halved,
    not as a large absolute count.
  - pbuf_def_total_count = 9984 corroborates the doc's port-side figure exactly
    (doc: "ours is 9,984"), which is the MEDIUM profile's NSS-side descriptor
    pool with extra_pbuf_core0 = 0.
